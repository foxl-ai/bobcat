\subsection{Sealed final: title selection by candidate count}

On the final split, \expone{} selects the source title with 94.7\% accuracy at $K=8$, 78.9\% at
$K=77$, 71.1\% at $K=200$ and 73.7\% at $K=255$. The report gives only the range for the
zero-shot model (47\% to 71\% across the four settings).
